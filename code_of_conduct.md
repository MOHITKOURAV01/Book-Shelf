# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Being patient with new contributors.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Collaborating openly and professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or offensive remarks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam or disruptive behavior.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.
- Removing or editing comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Communicating reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Code Reviews
- Project Documentation
- Community Events
- Any official communication channels related to this project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

**Contact:** maintainer@example.com

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially whenever possible.

Project maintainers will investigate every report fairly and take appropriate action.

---

## Enforcement

Examples of enforcement actions may include:

1. Warning
2. Temporary restriction from community interactions
3. Temporary suspension
4. Permanent ban from the project for repeated or severe violations

Maintainers reserve the right to determine appropriate actions based on the severity and frequency of violations.
